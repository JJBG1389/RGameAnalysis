# Team and Season Tranches

## Team Execution Capacity (TEC)

### T1 — Championship Elite
Consistent top-percentile teams with demonstrated championship-level execution.

**Default posture:** pursue physical limits, marginal advantages, sophisticated counter-meta capability, and championship alliance optimization.

### T2 — Championship Contender
Regular event winners/top alliance selections with credible championship upside.

**Default posture:** retain high ceiling while applying modest schedule/reliability constraints; optimize alliance compatibility.

### T3 — Regional Contender
Regular playoff contributor/first-pick caliber; occasional event winner.

**Default posture:** regional-winning architecture, strong autonomous, strategic versatility, controlled design risk.

### T4 — Emerging Competitor
Improving/playoff-capable team with inconsistent qualification performance.

**Default posture:** simpler high-ceiling architecture, earlier mechanical freeze, heavy emphasis on reliability, autonomous, contested performance, and driver repetitions.

### T5 — Developing
Sporadic playoff success with material capability/reliability gaps.

**Default posture:** one highly reliable primary scoring capability plus simple high-value secondary/endgame functions only when mature.

### T6 — Foundation
Rookie, rebuilding, or consistently low-performing team.

**Default posture:** legal robot, reliable drivetrain, one repeatable scoring capability, basic autonomous, maximum practice time.

## Trajectory

Attach one of:

- Rising
- Stable
- Declining

Trajectory affects acceptable development risk and tranche-transition targets.

## Season stages

### G1 — Kickoff
Rules-only analysis. Generate candidate archetypes and tranche-specific ceilings. Identify high-value unknowns.

### G2 — Prototype
Test uncertainties that can change architecture. Use Value of Information to decide what to prototype.

### G3 — Integration
Delete marginal capabilities. Move core functions through Exists → Reliable → Integrated. Apply tranche-specific design freeze.

### G4 — Event 1
Optimize qualification consistency, RP/advancement, autonomous reliability, pit turnaround, and data collection.

### G5 — Event 2 / Qualification
Use measured deficits to choose the single highest-value upgrade or process change. Avoid broad redesign without strong evidence.

### G6 — Championship / Late Season
Optimize playoff contribution, alliance complementarity, CPR, and opponent-specific counter-strategy.

## Decision matrix

Recommendations are generated from:

**Game × TEC Tranche × Trajectory × Season Stage × measured capability maturity**

This prevents the model from recommending a T1 development program to a T4–T6 team simply because the theoretical robot has a higher ceiling.
